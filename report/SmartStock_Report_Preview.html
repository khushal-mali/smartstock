Chapter 1: Introduction

In the contemporary commercial ecosystem, retail businesses, supply chains, and warehousing units face stringent operational demands requiring real-time visibility, rigorous accountability, and precise stock tracking. Traditional inventory control in small-to-medium retail enterprises (SMEs) and localized warehouses has predominantly relied on manual paper-based ledger registers or uncoupled, static spreadsheets. While these manual methods historically offered a nominal barrier to entry, they inherently suffer from critical systemic vulnerabilities: human transcription errors, delayed data synchronization, lack of concurrency controls, untracked stock shrinkage, and an acute vulnerability to out-of-stock scenarios.

The project, titled “SmartStock – Inventory Management System”, has been conceptualized, architected, and engineered to eradicate these systemic inefficiencies. Developed as an advanced three-tier web application, SmartStock centralizes product catalogs, category hierarchies, verified supplier profiles, procurement restocking (stock inflow), and point-of-sale billing (stock outflow) into a cohesive, transactionally safe software platform.

1.1 Technology Stack & Three-Tier Architecture

  • Frontend Tier: Next.js 16 (React 19, App Router) with Tailwind CSS and Lucide Icons.
  • Backend Tier: NestJS (Node.js LTS, TypeScript) REST API with modular architecture.
  • Database & ORM Tier: PostgreSQL 18 with Prisma 6 ORM executing atomic ACID transactions.
